# smelting-power-map

Siting and cell-design workspace for a flexible-load aluminium smelter.

- `index.html`: Voya Flex Smelter Siting Map. Self-contained Leaflet page
  ranking markets and sites by how long a flexible potline must stop and what
  that flexibility is worth versus a firm Hall-Héroult load.
- `research/power_modulation/`: condensed extracts of 14 papers, a thesis and
  a patent on Hall-Héroult cell behaviour under power modulation. Start at
  `research/power_modulation/INDEX.md`. The benchmark to beat is the TRIMET
  Essen EnPot potline (document 09 in the archive).
- `research/inert_anodes/`: condensed extracts of 28 documents on
  oxygen-evolving anodes, low-temperature KF-NaF-AlF3 electrolytes and oxide
  solubility in cryolite. Start at `research/inert_anodes/INDEX.md`.
