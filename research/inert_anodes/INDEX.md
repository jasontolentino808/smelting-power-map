# Inert-anode and low-temperature-electrolyte archive: index

Condensed extracts of 28 source documents on oxygen-evolving (inert) anodes,
low-temperature KF-NaF-AlF3 electrolytes, and the solubility and transport of
transition-metal oxides in cryolite. See `README.md` in this folder for how the
archive was produced and its limits (extractive digests, auto-cropped figures,
no digitised curves). Open `START_HERE.html` for per-document links.

This is a separate subject from `../power_modulation/`. It bears on which
anode and electrolyte a new cell uses, not on how a carbon-anode cell rides
through current changes.

## Caveats

- Documents 01 and 18 are not aluminium-electrolysis papers. 01 is nuclear
  molten-salt corrosion; 18 is a slag system. They were in the source folder
  and are kept, but weigh them accordingly.
- Several scanned sources (12, 16, 28) have OCR damage in the text extracts;
  the figure galleries are more reliable for those.
- No digitised curves. Solubility and conductivity values must be read from
  the source PDFs before use in a model.
