CeO2 in cryolite-alumina melts and interpreted them in
terms of the formation in solution of the species CeF3 and
CeOF. This assignment was based on the variation of the
solubility with the alumina content and a relation between
alumina content and alumina activity[2,3] derived from elec-
trochemical measurements.
